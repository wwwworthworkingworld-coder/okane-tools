Japan tax & pension guides for foreign residents

Short, plain-English guides for people who live in Japan or are about to leave. Every fact is checked against an official source and dated.

Leaving Japan

  • Leaving Japan: tax and pension checklist — what to do, in what order, before your flight.
  • Lump-sum Withdrawal Payment (pension refund) — who can claim, documents, the 20.42% tax and how to get it back.
  • Lump-sum Withdrawal Payment estimator — a free calculator using the official table.
  • Tax agent (nozei kanrinin) explained — what it is, who can be one, what the form asks.
  • Resident tax when you leave Japan — the January 1 rule and your options.
These pages explain how the official procedures work in general. They do not tell you what you should do in your own case. For that, ask the tax office, the Japan Pension Service, your city office, or a licensed tax accountant (zeirishi).

Why this site

Most guides on these topics are blog posts that mix rules with opinions. Here each page says what the official page says, shows the page you can check, and gives the date we checked it.
