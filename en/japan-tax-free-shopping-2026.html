Japan tax-free shopping from 1 November 2026

The tax-free system changed. You now pay the full price in the shop and get the consumption tax back when you leave. The process, from the official pages, checked 2026-11-17.

What changed

  • For purchases on or after 1 November 2026, tax-free shops charge the tax-inclusive price. Before that date, the tax was taken off at the shop.
  • The consumption tax is refunded after customs confirms at departure that the goods are leaving Japan.
  • Older guides and videos describe the previous system.

Step by step

  1. Shop at a store with a tax-free sign. You need ¥5,000 or more per day at the same store, before tax. Show your passport. Keep all receipts and the goods.
  2. Do not use or consume the goods in Japan. Food, drinks and cosmetics that you consume in Japan are not eligible. If you have, tell a customs officer at the counter.
  3. At departure, within 90 days of purchase and before you check in baggage, scan your passport at a tax-free procedure terminal in the departure lobby. Have all the goods with you.
  4. Result: a green result means no inspection and the procedure is complete. A red result means taking the goods to the customs inspection area. Finish before boarding.
  5. Refund: it comes by the method you registered with the shop or its refund agent. The official pages do not set one method; ask the shop.

Plan extra time at the airport. Neither the airline nor customs makes up for a missed flight.

Good to know

  • Visit Japan Web: at Narita, Haneda, Kansai, Chubu, Fukuoka, New Chitose and Naha you can start the procedure online instead of at a terminal. It is for travelers with "Temporary Visitor", "Diplomat" or "Official" status.
  • Each receipt is checked separately. If one item from a receipt is missing, none of that receipt's items qualify.
  • Not eligible: gold and platinum bullion, and items already exempt from consumption tax.
  • If goods that got customs confirmation are not exported, the equivalent tax is collected and penalties may apply.
  • Purchases must be things you can personally carry out of Japan.
