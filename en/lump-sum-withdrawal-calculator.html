A rough estimate from the official table. It runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Estimate only. Uses the Japan Pension Service tables for a last paid or insured month from April 2026 to March 2027 (National Pension), and the rates for a final month of April 2021 or later (Employees' Pension). If your last month is in another fiscal year, amounts differ. The JPS decides the real figure.
