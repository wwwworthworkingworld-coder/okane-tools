Tax agent (nozei kanrinin) explained

If you leave Japan but still have tax matters here, you appoint a tax agent. This is how the official pages describe it.

What it is

A tax agent is a person or company with an address in Japan who handles your national tax procedures while you are abroad. The NTA says: if you leave Japan and still have to deal with national tax, appoint a tax agent who is a resident in Japan. The agent can be a Japanese corporation or an individual residing in Japan.

Who can be one

  • The Japan Pension Service says there is no particular qualification other than having an address or place of residence in Japan.
  • Acting as your tax agent (handling your procedures) is different from giving tax advice or preparing tax documents for others. Those are reserved for licensed tax accountants (zeirishi). See the NTA's Q&A on the Tax Accountant Act (Japanese).

When to submit

  • Submit the "Notification of Tax Agent for Income Tax/Consumption Tax" to the tax office that covers your place of tax payment, before you leave.
  • If you forgot, JPS says you can submit it together with the return.
  • With the notification in place before departure, the return and payment for the year you leave go through your agent, normally between February 16 and March 15 of the next year.
  • The filing office is decided by your place of tax payment, not the agent's address.

What the agent does

According to the NTA's guide to the form, an appointed tax agent (except for objections and appeals) handles: filing returns, applications, requests and notifications and preparing and submitting the documents for them; receiving documents the tax office sends; and paying tax and receiving refunds.

What the form asks

The official form is titled "Notification of appointment/dismissal of a tax agent for income tax and consumption tax" (shotokuzei / shohizei no nozei kanrinin no sennin / kainin todokedesho). It is in Japanese. Its main parts:

PartWhat you write
TopTax office you submit to, date, your My Number (12 digits), name, date of birth, occupation, tax payment place (home, residence or business address) and phone
1Type of notification: appointment or dismissal; which tax (income tax, consumption tax)
2The agent: address, name, date of birth, phone, relationship to you, occupation
3Your address or residence abroad (for an appointment)
4The reason for appointing or dismissing
5Other: planned departure date and planned return date, and the kinds of income you will have in Japan (business, real estate, salary, capital gains, other)

When you submit it, the NTA guide says you must write your My Number and show ID or attach a copy. Submit it to the tax office that covers your place of tax payment. Cross out the words that do not apply (for example "consumption tax"). Use the current form from the NTA website (form ID NTA1TEZ240010030, checked on the date above); layouts change.

Choosing an agent, and what you owe, depend on your case. Ask the tax office or a licensed zeirishi. We do not act as anyone's tax agent.
